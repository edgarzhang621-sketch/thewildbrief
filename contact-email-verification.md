# Public Contact Email Verification

On 2026-08-25, the homepage footer was checked at 1280px desktop width and 375px iPhone width. The contact address is visible and readable at both sizes without clipping or horizontal overflow. TypeScript validation, all eight backend tests, and the production build passed after the update.
