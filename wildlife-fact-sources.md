# Wildlife Fact Sources

The homepage wildlife facts use the following verified public sources.

1. **World Wildlife Fund, 2024 Living Planet Report** — The report states that wildlife populations declined by an average of 73% since 1970. This refers to the average change in monitored wildlife population sizes; it is not a count of species lost. Source: https://www.worldwildlife.org/publications/2024-living-planet-report/

2. **UN Environment Programme / IPBES Global Assessment** — The assessment states that around 1 million animal and plant species are threatened with extinction, many within decades. Source: https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/natures-dangerous-decline-unprecedented-species-extinction-rates

3. **UN Environment Programme / Convention on Migratory Species, State of the World’s Migratory Species (2024)** — Nearly half (44%) of species listed under the Convention on Migratory Species are showing population declines. Source: https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/landmark-un-report-worlds-migratory-species-animals-are-decline-and
